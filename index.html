About Us

Crafting Luxury Moments Since 2014

Aura Decor Studio is a premier event decoration company based in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, specializing in creating breathtaking experiences. From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, we bring your vision to life with unparalleled elegance.
